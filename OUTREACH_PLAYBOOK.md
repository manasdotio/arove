# Velis Studio — SMB Outreach Playbook & Sales Strategy

This playbook outlines the exact strategies, cold templates, and calling scripts for targeting and acquiring **Small and Medium-Sized Businesses (SMBs)**. 

Unlike enterprise brands, SMBs do not buy "brand systems" or "experience design." They buy **more leads, phone calls, bookings, and trust**. This guide is designed to align your outreach with their direct business pain points.

---

## 1. Defining the SMB Service & Pricing Model

To win local business clients, you must align your offerings and pricing with their cash flow and decision-making speed.

### A. Shift in Positioning
* **Enterprise Focus:** Custom brand identity, storytelling, animations, modular design systems.
* **SMB Focus:** "Speed, Mobile Optimization, Local SEO, and Lead Generation." Explain that their website should be an automated sales representative, not just a digital brochure.

### B. Suggested Packages & Pricing
We recommend shifting the budget selector on the landing page from enterprise-tier pricing (`$10k - $50k+`) to SMB-friendly tiers:

| Tier | Package | Price Range | Perfect For | Key Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | **Local Landing Page** | $1,200 – $2,000 | Contractors, Salons, local gyms | 1-page high-converting design, Google Maps integration, click-to-call. |
| **Tier 2** | **Growth Website** | $2,000 – $4,500 | Law firms, medical clinics, multi-location services | 5-page custom site, basic SEO, booking integrations, speed optimization. |
| **Tier 3** | **Monthly Subscription** | $150 – $299/mo | Any local business wanting zero upfront cost | Built-in maintenance, monthly content updates, basic SEO, hosting included. |

## 2. Lead Sourcing Tactic: The "Low-Hanging Fruit"

Don't search blindly. Look for businesses that are already spending money but have subpar websites.

1. **Google Ads Search:** Search for keywords like `Plumber near me`, `Dentist [City]`, `Roofing contractor [City]`. If a business is paying for Google Ads but their landing page is slow, outdated, or not mobile-friendly, they are wasting ad spend. This is your highest-converting lead.
2. **Google Maps (3-Pack):** Look for businesses ranked 4th to 10th on Google Maps. They want to rank higher. A fast, SEO-optimized website will help them get into the coveted top 3.
3. **Broken Mobile Sites:** Over 60% of local searches happen on mobile. Visit local websites on your phone. If the header is broken, the phone number isn't clickable, or text overflows, you have an immediate hook.

## 3. Cold Email & Message Templates (Value-First)

**Rule #1 of Cold Outreach:** Never ask for a sale in the first message. Ask for permission to send value.

### Template 1: The "Mobile Layout Bug" (High Response Rate)
*Best for: Instagram DM, LinkedIn, or Email when you spot a specific layout issue.*

> **Subject:** Quick question about your website / mobile layout
> 
> Hi [Owner Name],
> 
> I was looking up [Service Type] in [City] on my phone and came across [Business Name]. 
> 
> I noticed that on mobile, the main button to call your team overflows the screen, making it hard for customers to click and get in touch. 
> 
> I took a quick 60-second screen recording showing where the bug is and how to fix it. Would it be alright if I sent that video over?
> 
> Best,
> 
> [Your Name]  
> Velis Studio

* **Why it works:** It is highly personalized, identifies a real problem that is costing them business, and doesn't ask for a meeting yet—only permission to send a helpful video.

### Template 2: The "Lighthouse Speed Audit" (PageSpeed Hook)
*Best for: Emailing businesses running paid Google Ads.*

> **Subject:** Your Google Ad landing page speed

> 
> Would you be open to seeing the preview link? No strings attached.
> 
> Best,
> 
> [Your Name]  
> Velis Studio

## 4. Cold Calling Script (The Value Hook)

Local business owners are busy. Your pitch must be short, respectful of their time, and focused on value.

### Step 1: The Opener (Permission & Respect)
> *"Hi [Owner's Name], my name is [Your Name] from Velis Studio. I know you're probably busy running [Business Name], so I’ll be brief. Do you have 45 seconds?"*

### Step 2: The Observation (The Hook)
> *"I was looking at your website today, and I saw that you have fantastic 5-star reviews on Google, but they aren't shown on your homepage. 
> 
> In our experience, local businesses lose about 30% of their site visitors because they don't showcase that trust immediately."*

### Step 3: The Soft Offer
> *"We build ultra-fast, mobile-friendly sites for local businesses like yours to help turn web visitors into actual phone calls. 
> 
> I’ve actually prepared a quick, 2-minute visual audit of your website showing three spots where you might be losing potential leads. 
> 
> Can I send that video to your email or text it to your phone?"*

### Step 4: The Close
> *"Great, what’s the best email/number to send that to? ... Awesome, I'll send it over in 10 minutes. If you like the suggestions, we can chat; if not, you can hand the video to your current web guy to fix it. Fair enough?"*
